DIRECT EVIDENCE, verbatim from the June 17, 2026 press conference transcript:
  "It's been the practice of this Committee for participants to submit these projections, and
   I have encouraged my colleagues to continue to do so. I, however, have refrained from
   offering any projections of my own, consistent with my long-held views on the SEP, at
   least as currently structured."
  and, answering Michael McKee on the dot plot:
  "I did not submit a, a dot. For me, it's not helpful in the conduct of policy."

THE ARITHMETIC NOW CLOSES EXACTLY:
  March 2026 SEP = 19 participants
    7 governors (Powell, Barr, Bowman, Cook, Jefferson, Waller, Miran) + 12 districts = 19  OK
  June 2026 SEP = 18 participants
    7 governors (Warsh, Barr, Bowman, Cook, Jefferson, Powell, Waller) MINUS Warsh = 6
    + 12 districts (incl. Atlanta/Venable) = 18  OK

  If Atlanta were NOT submitting, June would be 17, not 18. It is 18. Therefore Atlanta
  submits and Warsh does not.

inferred the opposite - that Atlanta was the missing submitter - from the minutes attendance

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR SCORING:
  1. The June 2026 median dot EXCLUDES THE CHAIR. Any model that treats the SEP median as

  3. Warsh has also signalled the SEP itself may change: the communications task force will
     "propose some well-considered changes, including to the SEP."

ADDENDUM 2026-09-02 — Venable's own words on her FOMC standing
================================================================
From her 2026-08-11 quarterly essay (Atlanta Fed, "Messages from the
President"), verbatim:

  "While the Atlanta Reserve Bank president is not a voting member this
   year, I am a full participant around the meeting table in Washington,
   DC. It has been fascinating to be a part of the discussions and
   contribute my thoughts as the FOMC representative for the people of
   our six-state district."

Why this matters for item 25 ("Atlanta interim president — confirm
whether she submits SEP projections"):

"Participant" is the FOMC's own term of art. The SEP is compiled from
the projections of PARTICIPANTS — all governors plus all twelve Reserve
Bank presidents — not from VOTERS. Venable identifies herself as a full
participant and as "the FOMC representative" for the Sixth District.
That is the category that submits SEP projections.

This is consistent with, and independent of, the arithmetic already
recorded above (which resolved the missing June-2026 submitter as
WARSH, not Atlanta). Two independent lines of evidence now agree:
Venable submits.
