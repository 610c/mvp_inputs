ITEM 23: Board committee assignments

STATUTORY BACKGROUND (verbatim)
The seven members of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System are nominated by the
President and confirmed by the Senate. A full term is fourteen years. One term begins every two
years, on February 1 of even-numbered years. A member who serves a full term may not be
reappointed. A member who completes an unexpired portion of a term may be reappointed. All terms
end on their statutory date regardless of the date on which the member is sworn into office.
The Chairman and the Vice Chair of the Board, as well as the Vice Chair for Supervision, are
nominated by the President from among the members and are confirmed by the Senate. They serve a
term of four years. A member's term on the Board is not affected by his or her status as
Chairman or Vice Chair.

NOTE FOR SCORING (item 22/23 "clout"):
  Warsh holding no committee seat is normal for a sitting Chairman.
  POWELL HOLDING NO COMMITTEE SEAT IS NOT. He remains a sitting governor and an FOMC voter
  (confirmed in the June and July minutes rosters), but carries no Board committee
  responsibility and no oversight-governor portfolio. Every other non-Chair governor holds
  three or more. On a structural-influence measure he is a voting member with zero
  institutional portfolio - an unusual configuration worth encoding explicitly rather than
  letting a committee-count metric treat him as a normal governor.
