3. LISA COOK — STATUS: IN OFFICE. LITIGATION ACTIVE.
================================================================
Cook is a sitting governor as of 2026-09-02. She appears on the current
Board roster, chairs the Committee on Financial Stability, sits on three
other Board committees, and gave three speeches inside the corpus window
(2026-06-24, 2026-07-15, 2026-08-05).

THE FACTS, from CRS R48233 (see CRS_R48233_board_membership.txt):
  Appointed by President Biden to the unexpired term previously held by
  Janet Yellen; reappointed to a full term, Senate confirmed 2025-09-06.
  2025-08-25  President Trump sent Cook a letter stating she was removed
              from office "effective immediately."
  2025-08-28  Cook filed suit challenging the purported removal.
  2025-09-15  An appeals court rejected the request to remove her
              immediately (WSJ, per CRS footnote 10).
              The Federal Reserve stated it would abide by the court ruling.
  CRS, verbatim: "Cook remains in office for the time being as legal
  proceedings in the [case continue]."

THE STATUTE AT ISSUE, verbatim from CRS:
  Fed governors are appointed "for a term of fourteen years from the
  expiration of the term of his predecessor, unless sooner REMOVED FOR
  CAUSE BY THE PRESIDENT." (12 U.S.C. 242)

STATUS OF THE LITIGATION AS OF CAPTURE: unresolved. CRS R48233 reports the
case as still proceeding as of the report's publication. I found no more
recent primary-source update — the Board's 2026 press-release index says
nothing about it, and the Board does not comment on the matter publicly.

WHY THIS IS A LIVE RISK TO THE MODEL, not just background:
Cook is one of seven governors and a full FOMC voter at every 2026 meeting.
She voted WITH the majority at every meeting in the record. If the
litigation resolves against her mid-sample, the Committee loses a voter and
the vote arithmetic changes. More subtly: a governor under active threat of
removal has an unusual incentive structure around dissenting, in either
direction, and a dissent-propensity model that treats all seven governors
as facing identical costs is mis-specified for Cook specifically.

5. THE CHAIRMAN'S TASK FORCES — 2026-07-09
   *** THE BIGGEST STANDING RISK TO THIS ENTIRE PROJECT ***
================================================================
Six weeks into his chairmanship Warsh announced five task forces, co-led by
external advisers, "with a mandate to follow the evidence, provide candid
feedback, and produce rigorous findings for the Federal Open Market
Committee."  [monetary20260709a.htm]

  COMMUNICATIONS — "Review how the Federal Reserve conveys policy
  DELIBERATIONS AND DECISIONS amid uncertainty."
      Peter R. Fisher, professor of practice, Foster School of Business,
        University of Washington
      Arminio Fraga, founder and chairman, Gávea Investimentos;
        former president, Central Bank of Brazil
      MERVYN KING, former governor, BANK OF ENGLAND

  BALANCE SHEET POLICY — Karen Dynan; Raghuram Rajan; Jeremy Stein
  DATA — Raj Chetty; Doug McMillon; Kevin Murphy
  PRODUCTIVITY AND JOBS — Marc Andreessen; Charles I. Jones; Asha Sharma
  INFLATION FRAMEWORKS — Greg Mankiw; Thomas Sargent; William White

Warsh, verbatim: "Each task force will carefully consider whether
policymakers' means and methods, analytical tools and policy approaches can
be improved upon."

WHY THIS IS THE HEADLINE RISK:
Your dependent variable is built from how the Fed publishes disagreement —
the "Voting against this action" line in the statement, the participant
counts in the minutes, the dots in the SEP. A task force has now been
chartered, by name, to review exactly that, and it is co-led by a former
Bank of England governor. The Chairman who commissioned it is the same
person who wrote the 2014 review that changed the Bank of England's
disclosure of MPC deliberations, and who has said publicly that the Fed
should "talk a lot less."
  See ../08_warsh/warsh_2014_boe_mpc_transparency_review.txt

CONCRETE FAILURE MODE: if the statement stops carrying a policy-bias
sentence, the entire April-2026 dissent type disappears — Hammack,
Kashkari and Logan dissented on the BIAS LANGUAGE, not the rate. Three of
the four dissents in the largest dissent event of 2026 exist only because
of a sentence a communications review could delete. That would read in the
data as three participants becoming compliant. It would not be that.

RECOMMENDATION: treat any change in FOMC communication practice as a
STRUCTURAL BREAK to be dated and controlled for, not as behaviour. Watch
"More information on the task forces and topics will be posted periodically
on the Federal Reserve's website" and the Related Content link
"Chairman's Task Forces for Advancing Monetary Policy."
