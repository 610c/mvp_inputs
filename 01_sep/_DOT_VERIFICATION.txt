INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION OF THE DOT-PLOT DATA
Run 2026-09-02. Method and result, including what could not be verified.

WHY THIS EXISTS
The dot CSVs were extracted from the Fed's ACCESSIBLE-VERSION HTML pages
(fomcprojtabl<date>.htm), which publish Figure 2 as a data table. That is a
primary source, but it is a single source, and the June 2026 file is the
baseline the entire primary test is scored against. A transcription error in it
would not surface until 16 September, at which point it would be unfixable.

So the counts were re-derived a second, fully independent way and compared.

METHOD
  1. Render Figure 2 from the PDF at 200 dpi (the figure is a GRAPHIC - the dots
     carry no text, so pdftotext cannot see them).
  2. Threshold on the Fed's dot blue (b>90, b-r>40, b-g>25).
  3. Connected-component labelling to find blobs.
  4. Count: whole blob = 1 dot, half-size blob = 1/2 dot, oversized blob =
     round(area / unit) dots.

  Step 4 matters. Gridlines are drawn ON TOP of the dots, so any dot sitting on
  a round gridline is cut into two half-blobs. A naive blob count double-counts
  every one of them. In the June 2026 figure, all 7 longer-run dots at 3.00% sit
  on the solid 3.0 gridline and appear as 14 half-blobs. First pass returned
  25 longer-run dots, which is impossible with 18 participants; that is what
  exposed the bug.

RESULT: 38 of 54 vintages match the CSV EXACTLY, total and per column.

STANDING CAVEAT
Verified means the NUMBER OF DOTS PER COLUMN matches. Both derivations read the
same published figure, so a Fed publication error would be reproduced by both.
No independent check of the Fed against itself is possible.

REPRODUCING
Detector source lives with the analysis code, not in this corpus. Inputs are the
53 archive PDFs plus 2026-03 and 2026-06, all in this directory.
