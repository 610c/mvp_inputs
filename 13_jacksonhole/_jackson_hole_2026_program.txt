WHAT THE PROGRAM ITSELF TELLS YOU — read this before scoring
================================================================

1. EXACTLY TWO FOMC PARTICIPANTS APPEAR ON THE 2026 PROGRAM.
   Warsh (keynote) and Schmid (host of the opening dinner, in his capacity
   as the hosting Bank's president — a ceremonial slot, not a policy talk).
   NO other governor and NO other Reserve Bank president has a speaking
   role. Every author, discussant, chair and panelist is an academic or a
   foreign/international official.

   This matters for salience scoring in two ways:
   (a) Jackson Hole is often treated as a high-weight venue. In 2026 it
       contributes ONE monetary-policy communication from a US policymaker,
       not a cluster of them. Do not inflate a district president's
       salience score on the assumption they "spoke at Jackson Hole" —
       attending is not speaking, and the program is the evidence.
   (b) Warsh's keynote is therefore unusually load-bearing. It is the only
       Fed policy statement made at the event, delivered on 2026-08-28,
       six days before the corpus window closes and roughly three weeks
       before the September FOMC meeting.

2. THE 2026 THEME IS NOT ABOUT THE POLICY STANCE.
   "Financial Innovation: Implications for Payments and Policy" — the
   papers are on tokenized finance, the international monetary system,
   the future of banking, and financing innovation. A researcher expecting
   the usual Jackson Hole inflation/labour-market fare will find almost
   none of it in the academic program. Warsh's keynote is where the
   stance content is, and it is thematically separate from the rest of
   the agenda.

3. ATTENDANCE IS NOT PUBLISHED.
   The Kansas City Fed publishes the PROGRAM, not the attendee list. Fed
   presidents and governors who attended but did not speak leave no trace
   in this source. Several gave media interviews from Jackson Hole that
   are captured elsewhere in this corpus (see 05_speeches/districts/) —
   those interviews, not the program, are the evidence of their presence.
   Collins (Boston) confirms her own attendance in her 2026-08-25
   Perspectives essay: "Before heading to the Federal Reserve's annual
   symposium at Jackson Hole, Wyoming..."
   (../05_speeches/districts/boston/collins_20260825_perspectives_on_the_economy.txt)

4. "GENERAL DISCUSSION" SESSIONS ARE NOT TRANSCRIBED IN REAL TIME.
   The agenda lists a General Discussion after every paper and panel.
   These are published later, in the bound symposium proceedings volume,
   typically months after the event. As of 2026-09-02 they are not
   available. This is the same structural gap documented for item 19
   (Q&A following posted speeches): the most revealing exchanges are the
   ones that go unpublished within the scoring window.
